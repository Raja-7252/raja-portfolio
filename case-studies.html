PBM Operations · Bristol Myers Squibb

Faster Approvals, Shorter Onboarding

First-pass approvals 62% → 89% · Patient onboarding 14 → 6 days

The Problem

Across 12 specialty pharmacy partners, first-pass approval rates were stuck at 62% and patient onboarding dragged on for 14 days. The bottlenecks were invisible — buried in workflows no one had measured — and leaders had no dashboard to see where approvals stalled.

What I Did

  • Built advanced Excel models and Tableau dashboards tracking first-pass approval rates by payer.
  • Analyzed workflow bottlenecks and PBM operational data across all 12 partners to pinpoint root causes.
  • Implemented process improvements targeting the highest-impact steps in the onboarding path.
  • Created financial and operational metrics supporting revenue maximization initiatives.
  • Transferred the dashboards and methodology to internal teams so monitoring continues.

The Outcome

First-pass approval performance climbed from 62% to 89%, and patient onboarding time dropped from 14 to 6 days — a 57% reduction. Faster onboarding means faster revenue recognition and a better member experience, and the dashboards make every payer's performance visible at a glance.

Claims Analytics · Mphasis (Medicare/Medicaid)

Cutting Claims Rejections in Half

Rejection rates 22% → 12% · Two healthcare clients supported

The Problem

For Medicare and Medicaid plans, claims rejections at 22% meant revenue leaks and frustrated members. Claims edits and denial management were run on scattered spreadsheets with no single view of what was being rejected — or why.

What I Did

  • Developed Power BI and advanced Excel reporting tracking claims edits, denial management, and revenue-cycle metrics.
  • Analyzed rejection patterns to identify fixable edit and submission issues.
  • Designed financial and operational reporting models supporting PBM decision-making.
  • Coordinated analytics across two healthcare clients concurrently, sharing methodology and best practices.

The Outcome

Claims rejection rates dropped from 22% to 12%, improving claims accuracy and protecting revenue. The Power BI foundation gave teams a single source of truth — the same discipline I bring to every engagement today.
