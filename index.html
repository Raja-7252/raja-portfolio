About Me

Analytical rigor, human perspective.

A PBM analytics professional who cares about the people behind the numbers.

I'm a Senior Business Analyst with 4+ years turning complex healthcare data into financial impact. At Bristol Myers Squibb, my Excel models and Tableau dashboards lifted first-pass approvals from 62% to 89% and cut patient onboarding from 14 to 6 days. At Mphasis, my Power BI reporting helped reduce claims rejections from 22% to 12% for Medicare/Medicaid plans.

My cost-avoidance analysis identified $4,200 per-patient savings, supporting three major payer contract renewals. With a 4.0 GPA Master's in Computer Engineering and AWS Cloud Practitioner certification, I bring both the methodology and the follow-through — and I make sure the improvements stick by transferring them to the teams who use them daily.

"I got into healthcare analytics because I saw what bad data costs real people — time, money, and trust. My job is making sure that stops."
